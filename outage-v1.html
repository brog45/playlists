Outage v1

The five stages of grief, as experienced by an on-call engineer during a major outage.
Inspired by the M365 outage on Nov 12, 2026.

https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLH9qAzCgaCgs

Prologue

  • "Well, the network’s down." (Ameritech Data ad)
    "Bagel?"

  • Wake Up (Rage Against the Machine)
    The pager goes off at 2 a.m. Six minutes long, because snooze is not an option.

  • Production (The Wiseguys)
    "Which environment?"

Denial

  • Enjoy the Silence (Depeche Mode)
    The dashboards are quiet. Too quiet. (Monitoring is down too.)

  • Breathe (Télépopmusik)
    "It’s probably just DNS propagation. Everybody breathe."

  • drama free (feat. Lights) (deadmau5)
    Status page still says "All Systems Operational."

Anger

  • F**k the System (System of a Down)
    The whole system. All of it.

  • Burn It Down (AWOLNATION)
    Someone in the war room proposes nuking the cluster from orbit.

  • Timebomb Zone (The Prodigy)
    "But nobody’s touched this code since 2014!"

Bargaining

  • Never Let Me Down Again (Depeche Mode)
    Pleading with the failover cluster. And the backups.

  • Voodoo (Godsmack)
    Clear the caches, restart the services, sacrifice a chicken.

  • Techno Shaman (Ummet Ozcan)
    Summon the one engineer who still understands the legacy stack.

  • Move Any Mountain (The Shamen)
    Promise the vendor anything: a multi-year renewal, a case study, your firstborn.

  • Cthulhu Sleeps (deadmau5)
    The ancient mainframe in the basement. Do not wake it. (Ten and a half minutes of dread.)

Depression

  • Stress in a Box (International Thief Thief)
    Hour four of the bridge call.

  • nosedive (deadmau5)
    Every graph on the dashboard. Revenue too.

  • Confusion (New Order)
    Fourteen people on the bridge, fourteen theories.

Acceptance

  • Race Condition (Stereo Drift)
    On occasion, it’s actually not DNS!

  • It’s the End of the World as We Know It (R.E.M.)
    ...and I feel fine. Ship it!

  • (Nothing But) Flowers (Talking Heads)
    Life after the collapse, missing the old monolith.

Epilogue

  • G.O.A.T. (Polyphia)
    Kudos in the incident channel for whoever found it.

  • The Game Has Changed (Daft Punk)
    The postmortem. New architecture, new runbooks.
