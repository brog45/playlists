## Prologue

* ["Well, the network's down."](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8wbL88f4gI) (Ameritech Data ad)  
"Bagel?"

* [Wake Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4lzqUe1Qfec) (Rage Against the Machine)  
The pager goes off at 2 a.m. Six minutes long, because snooze is not an option.

* [Production](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6cQo8YFH__Y) (The Wiseguys)  
"Which environment?"

## Denial

* [Enjoy the Silence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ojvldIzbaMo) (Depeche Mode)  
The dashboards are quiet. Too quiet. (Monitoring is down too.)

* [Breathe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vyut3GyQtn0) (Télépopmusik)  
"It's probably just DNS propagation. Everybody breathe."

* [drama free (feat. Lights)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3J2fkABpwBA) (deadmau5)  
Status page still says "All Systems Operational."

## Anger

* [F\*\*k the System](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AEyhEJdqyrw) (System of a Down)  
The whole system. All of it.

* [Burn It Down](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4y4TsJHgVU) (AWOLNATION)  
Someone in the war room proposes nuking the cluster from orbit.

* [Timebomb Zone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-5tHiZACxbI) (The Prodigy)  
"But nobody's touched this code since 2014!"

## Bargaining

* [Never Let Me Down Again](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=snILjFUkk_A) (Depeche Mode)  
Pleading with the failover cluster. And the backups.

* [Voodoo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9SSUQxGjZZ4) (Godsmack)  
Clear the caches, restart the services, sacrifice a chicken.

* [Techno Shaman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ooc8QR2WleM) (Ummet Ozcan)  
Summon the one engineer who still understands the legacy stack.

* [Move Any Mountain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PthnTuzKU3o) (The Shamen)  
Promise the vendor anything: a multi-year renewal, a case study, your firstborn.

* [Cthulhu Sleeps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=whbXCC33Pwc) (deadmau5)  
The ancient mainframe in the basement. Do *not* wake it. (Ten and a half minutes of dread.)

## Depression

* [Stress in a Box](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jag_5JCq-lY) (International Thief Thief)  
Hour four of the bridge call.

* [nosedive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zSxlnaF9jf0) (deadmau5)  
Every graph on the dashboard. Revenue too.

* [Confusion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7tVirVifUJk) (New Order)  
Fourteen people on the bridge, fourteen theories.

## Acceptance

* [Race Condition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg01iQ3xZtE) (Stereo Drift)

* [It's the End of the World as We Know It](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z0GFRcFm-aY) (R.E.M.)

* [(Nothing But) Flowers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2twY8YQYDBE) (Talking Heads)
